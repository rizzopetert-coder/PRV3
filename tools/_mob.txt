\\\# 1. What PRV3 Is

  

  

  

PRV3 is a diagnostic instrument for organizational friction. An internal leader with budget authority — the Principal — takes the instrument. The engine identifies their organizational state. The output names the condition precisely and routes to a resolution recommendation. The diagnostic is the product. Everything else serves it.

  

  

  

The diagnostic is the primary entry point. Four resolution services sit beneath it: Groundwork, Development, First Call, Advisory. Service names locked Session 32. Two-tier structure locked Session 33. Pricing not locked.

\*\*Shareable output design intent — locked Session 34:\*\* The shareable output is designed to travel without the user present. It enters rooms the practitioner isn’t in — a board meeting, a budget conversation, a leadership team discussion about whether to engage outside help. Its job is to carry the condition’s financial weight and behavioral precision into those rooms and do the persuasion work required to justify the engagement before the practitioner arrives. Friction Tax multipliers (sourced from McKinsey, SHRM, Gallup, and peer-reviewed research) give the condition economic visibility. Patterned consequence logic gives it behavioral precision. The combination makes the condition undeniable to a financially-oriented audience. The shareable output never names a specific service. It names the condition, the severity, and the resolution family. Service recommendation is made by the practitioner in the live consultation — not by the instrument. This boundary is architecturally enforced, not aspirational.

Scale mismatch between accumulated space and profile space handled natively by cosine normalization. answered\\\_question\\\_count parameter added to rank\\\_states() — dynamic session scalar applied at runtime. SALIENCE\\\_PROFILES weight vectors retained (target dimension fields: 2.5; off-axis: 0.4). Confusion Matrix methodology for Phase 1 calibration of dimensional salience weights.
