## Section 5 — Path to HC Pass Rate ≥15/47

The binding constraint is not the question library geometry — it is the absence of populated answers. `best_option_for_state()` is a noise generator relative to the signal routing problem. HC profiles on noise behave like moderate profiles on signal.

**The floor barrier:** Even with correct rank-1 routing, `paper_shield` simultaneously clears its floor (0.8785 × 1.00 = 0.8785) for the majority of profiles, producing `multi_state` output where `single_state` is required for HC pass. This is the direct mechanical cause of 0/47 HC pass.

**Two paths forward — Pete decides:**

**Path A — Answer population (primary unlock).** Populate the 142 test profile `answers` arrays with specific question-by-question selections derived from the state Signal Map. This converts noise profiles to signal profiles. Real answers suppress cross-dimensional contamination because states with strong Authority signals will not accumulate Attitude/Aptitude options. When answers are populated, contrast fields become active — their suppression of specific options fires only when those options are actually selected. Expected result: HC pass rate begins to diverge from noise-regime behavior.

**Path B — Vector sharpening (secondary unlock, diminishing returns).** Further differentiate `paper_shield` from the rest of the low-signal state group. Risk: another sink cascade to the next most absorptive state. Gemini flagged this as a structural trap. Not recommended without Path A data to validate.

**Recommendation:** Path A. Answer population is the stated Phase 2 objective. Contrast field injection (this session) clears the prerequisites for that work. The v11 calibration confirms the injection is structurally correct and did not break anything.
