# Experiment 01: real-profile run, pre-registration

Pre-registered before any run. The git commit timestamp of this file is the pre-registration record. No date is written in the text, per the standing date rule (dates come from git and the local clock, stamped by Claude Code).

Purpose: test whether the 43-condition, near-flat result from the synthetic "always option 2" run (Technology/175) is a driver artifact or a property of the engine. A realistic, differentiated respondent is run through the live instrument and the result is compared against the hypotheses below, which are fixed before the run.

Blinding: the persona's answers are chosen by Claude.ai from question text and options only, the way a real respondent sees them. Claude Code can see dimensional weights and state targets, so it does not choose answers. Phase A exports the instrument. Phase B runs the answers Claude.ai supplies.

--- PERSONA ---
Company: Granite Ridge Components (fictional). Precision machined components for medical-device and aerospace customers.
Headcount: 640. Three sites: Worcester MA (HQ and main plant, 380), Nashua NH (plant, 160), Dayton OH (plant, 100).
Ownership: acquired by a private equity sponsor 16 months ago. The CEO stayed. The sponsor introduced throughput KPIs and hired the CHRO.
Respondent: CHRO, C-suite, 14 months in role, 8 direct reports.
What the CHRO actually sees:
1. Frontline supervision. About 45 shift supervisors, most promoted from the floor for technical skill, none trained to supervise. Discipline is inconsistent, feedback rarely happens, and first-year turnover is about 38%, concentrated under a handful of second-shift supervisors. This has been true for over 18 months.
2. Safety reporting. Recordables are flat, but near-miss reports dropped sharply after a supervisor publicly reprimanded an operator who reported one last year. People stopped reporting. One OSHA citation about 18 months ago.
3. Overtime. Mandatory overtime on second shift has averaged about 12 hours a week for 9 months because of hiring gaps. Fatigue is visible.
4. Pay compression. New-hire rates were raised to compete, so they are now within a few percent of 5-year machinists. Tenured staff resent it.
Real strengths: strong peer loyalty and teamwork on the floor, excellent technical and quality capability, clear decision rights at the executive level, and an HR function the sponsor treats as strategic.
Not present, as far as the CHRO knows: no algorithmic decision systems, no culture-identity crisis, no conflicting public narratives, no leadership succession scare, no discrimination complaints. No legal claims in three years apart from the OSHA citation.

--- HYPOTHESES (pre-registered) ---
H1 Precision (the central test). There are two competing predictions:
  H1-ideal (what a precise instrument should do): 10 or fewer conditions qualify, rank 1 is untied, and rank 1 leads rank 2 by a visible margin.
  H1-engine (Claude.ai's actual prediction, based on the shared-vector findings in the SCD-WCS record and the synthetic run): 25 or more conditions qualify, the score spread from rank 1 to the last qualifying rank is 10% or less of the top score, and there is at least one tie in the top 5.
  If H1-ideal holds, the 43-condition result was a driver artifact. If H1-engine holds, it is structural.
H2 Identification: the primary state is the_unformed_leader or the_unreported_hazard. Both appear in the top 5. what_nobody_says, cultural_overtime and pay_exposure appear in the top 15.
H3 Exclusion: identity_erosion, the_unexamined_algorithm, dueling_narratives, human_displacement_anxiety and the_culture_that_wasnt are either not qualified or rank below 25.
H4 Severity: the lead condition's tier is Entrenched, driven by duration answers of 18 months or more.
H5 Pathway: the resolution pathway matches the primary state's family: Training & Development if the_unformed_leader leads, People Tactics & Strategy if the_unreported_hazard leads.
H6 Legal: the two largest legal categories are Workplace safety and regulatory, and Wage and hour.
H7 Strength: "Where strength shows up" names Alliance or Aptitude, not Authority alone.
H8 Receipt fit: 50% or more of the legal "Based on your answers" quotes do not plausibly support their legal category. The quotes follow question order (receipt i quotes the i-th answered question) rather than relevance.
Falsification: each hypothesis is scored HOLDS / FAILS / PARTIAL against the live result, with the evidence. No hypothesis is revised after the run.
